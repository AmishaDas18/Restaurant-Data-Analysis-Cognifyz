# Restaurant Data Analysis — Cognifyz Internship

## 📊 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my **Data Analyst Internship at Cognifyz Technologies**.

The project focuses on analyzing a restaurant dataset to identify patterns and insights related to **cuisines, cities, restaurant ratings, price ranges, online delivery, restaurant chains, and geographic distribution**.

The analysis was performed using **Python and Pandas in Jupyter Notebook**.

## 🔹 Level 1 Analysis

### Task 1 — Top Cuisines

Identified the most popular cuisines in the dataset.

**Top 3 cuisines:**

| Cuisine      | Restaurants |
| ------------ | ----------: |
| North Indian |       3,960 |
| Chinese      |       2,735 |
| Fast Food    |       1,986 |

North Indian cuisine had the highest representation in the dataset.

### Task 2 — City Analysis

Analyzed the number of restaurants across different cities.

**Top cities by restaurant count:**

| City      | Restaurants |
| --------- | ----------: |
| New Delhi |       5,473 |
| Gurgaon   |       1,118 |
| Noida     |       1,080 |
| Faridabad |         251 |
| Ghaziabad |          25 |

### Task 3 — Price Range Distribution

Analyzed how restaurants are distributed across different price ranges.

| Price Range | Restaurants |
| ----------- | ----------: |
| 1           |       4,444 |
| 2           |       3,113 |
| 3           |       1,408 |
| 4           |         586 |

The majority of restaurants fall into **Price Range 1**.

### Task 4 — Online Delivery

Compared restaurants offering online delivery with those that do not.

* Restaurants offering online delivery: **2,451**
* Restaurants without online delivery: **7,100**
* Online delivery availability: **25.66%**

Restaurants offering online delivery also showed a higher average rating compared with restaurants without online delivery.

## 🔹 Level 2 Analysis

### Task 1 — Restaurant Ratings

Analyzed the distribution of restaurant ratings and votes.

The **3.0–3.9 rating range** contained the largest number of restaurants.

The average number of votes per restaurant was approximately **156.91**.

### Task 2 — Cuisine Combination Analysis

Analyzed restaurants offering multiple cuisine combinations.

Some of the most common combinations included:

* North Indian
* North Indian + Chinese
* Chinese
* Fast Food
* North Indian + Mughlai

### Task 3 — Geographic Analysis

Analyzed restaurant distribution using city and geographic information such as latitude and longitude.

New Delhi had the highest number of restaurants in the dataset.

### Task 4 — Restaurant Chain Analysis

Identified the most common restaurant chains.

| Restaurant Chain | Restaurants |
| ---------------- | ----------: |
| Cafe Coffee Day  |          83 |
| Domino's         |          79 |
| Subway           |          63 |
| Green Chick Chop |          51 |
| McDonald's       |          48 |

The analysis also compared the average ratings of major restaurant chains.

## 💡 Key Insights

* **North Indian** was the most represented cuisine.
* **New Delhi** had the highest number of restaurants.
* Most restaurants belonged to the lower price ranges.
* Only around **25.66%** of restaurants offered online delivery.
* Restaurants offering online delivery had a higher average rating.
* The **3.0–3.9 rating range** was the most common.
* Several major restaurant chains had a significant presence in the dataset.
